Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16–18 September 2026 | Speke Resort Munyonyo, Kampala

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 is organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF). It brings together coffee farmers, roasters, buyers, investors and policy makers to showcase Uganda's world-class Arabica and Robusta coffees.

Over three days, visitors can attend talks, explore exhibition booths, taste premium coffees and build new business partnerships. Whether you grow, trade or simply love coffee, there is something here for you.
